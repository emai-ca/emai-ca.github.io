<section class="page-hero content-wide reveal">
  <p class="eyebrow">About the project / 01</p>
  <h1>Four ideas for<br><em>creative agency</em><span class="accent">.</span></h1>
  <p class="page-lede">Embodying AI: Music Making Beyond the Prompt (EMAI) is a four-year research-creation project investigating how musicians can exercise agency when working with machine learning systems in music composition and performance.</p>
</section>

<section class="content-wide split-section">
  <div class="section-label">The premise</div>
  <div class="rich-copy"><p>Large, text-prompt-driven models are powerful imitators, but their low-bandwidth interaction is poorly suited to sustained creative practice, live performance, and culturally diverse repertoires. EMAI develops and disseminates approaches centred on embodiment, small data, authorship, and literacy.</p><p>The project treats machine learning not as a replacement for human creativity, but as a set of tools and practices through which musicians can sustain cultural specificity, authorship, and intentionality.</p></div>
</section>

<section class="principles content-wide">
  <div class="principle"><span>01</span><h2>Embodiment<br><em>and expressivity</em></h2><p>Link gestures and multimodal input to generative processes so that performers can shape musical outcomes in real time.</p></div>
  <div class="principle"><span>02</span><h2>Small data,<br><em>small models</em></h2><p>Curate context-specific datasets and lightweight models that foreground provenance, transparency, and access.</p></div>
  <div class="principle"><span>03</span><h2>System<br><em>appropriation</em></h2><p>Interrogate how authorship is distributed across data curation, training, mappings, and performance.</p></div>
  <div class="principle"><span>04</span><h2>Documentation<br><em>and literacy</em></h2><p>Produce open datasets, models, code, methods, and reflective resources for artist-researchers.</p></div>
</section>

<section class="question-band"><div class="content-wide"><span>Central inquiry</span><h2>How can musicians sustain agency and artistic voice while building literacy around AI in creative practice?</h2></div></section>
